Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version: Radiant Wallet is a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Your recovery phrase is created and encrypted on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Radiant Wallet Android application (org.radiant.wallet), published by Birnie ("we", "us"). It applies only to that app; it does not cover websites or services you reach from it.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate a backend, and the app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, or advertising SDKs. We never receive your recovery phrase, your private keys, your balances, or any identifier for you or your device.

3. Data stored on your device

DataWhere and why
Your recovery phrase (seed) Encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) and stored in the app's private storage. If you enable biometric unlock, a second copy is encrypted by a hardware-backed key in the Android Keystore.
Preferences The Electrum server you picked and whether screenshots are allowed, in the app's private storage.

All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Delete wallet" in Settings, removes it. We cannot recover it for you — only your written recovery phrase can.

4. Network connections

To show balances and broadcast transactions, the app talks directly to public ElectrumX servers run by third parties (community operators and the Radiant project), over encrypted WebSocket connections.

Those servers necessarily see your IP address and the blockchain addresses you query. That is inherent to how lightweight wallets work, and it is why the app supports switching servers. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies, not ours. The app does not send your recovery phrase, keys, or passphrase to any server.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy
INTERNET To reach public ElectrumX servers.
USE_BIOMETRIC Only if you turn on biometric unlock, to unlock the wallet with your fingerprint or face. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never available to the app.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access, and collects no advertising identifiers.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on request. You exercise full control locally: view or export your recovery phrase in Settings, and delete all wallet data with "Delete wallet" or by uninstalling.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
